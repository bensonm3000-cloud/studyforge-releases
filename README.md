# StudyForge

A free Android study planner for students, built by an independent developer.

## Features
- Weekly timetable with classes that repeat every week
- Tasks with deadlines and reminders (1 or 2 days before)
- Study alarms that take over the whole screen, so they are hard to swipe away
- Focus timer
- Built-in PDF viewer with highlights and notes
- Scan notes to text (done on your phone)
- Export your timetable as a PDF

## Permissions the app asks for, and why
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet / network state | Sign-in and syncing your data to your account |
| Exact alarms | Study alarms and reminders at the right time |
| Full-screen intent | Shows the alarm over the lock screen |
| Notifications | Reminder notifications |
| Vibrate, wake lock | Alarm sound and vibration |
| Run at startup | Re-schedules your alarms after the phone restarts |
| Foreground service | Keeps an alarm ringing until you stop it |
| Install packages | Lets the app install its own updates when you allow it |

StudyForge works offline for planning. Signing in and syncing need internet.
